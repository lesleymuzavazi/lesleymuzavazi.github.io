About

I am a Master’s student in Analytics at Saint Louis University with a strong foundation in Accounting and over five years of experience in finance and data-driven decision-making. My background has equipped me with the ability to work with complex datasets, identify patterns, and translate data into actionable insights that support strategic business decisions.

I have developed skills across data analytics, financial analysis, and predictive modeling, with hands-on experience using tools such as Python, SQL, Excel, and Power BI. Through my coursework in analytics and hands-on projects, I have built a solid understanding of predictive analysis, including regression techniques, classification models, and data-driven forecasting to support decision-making in uncertain environments.

I am interested in applying analytics across a wide range of industries, including finance, healthcare, technology, retail, and public sector organizations. I am especially motivated by opportunities where data can be used to improve efficiency, optimize processes, and drive meaningful impact. My goal is to bridge the gap between data and decision-making by delivering insights that are both technically sound and practically valuable.
